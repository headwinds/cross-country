# TextArea

This is a template for creating a new story; but is a really story?! 

It's obviously easier to start a story which could be epic fiction or simply some point of interest like the fact that Lamborghini began by building state of the art tractors; much harder to finish it.

With the help of AI, it has become much easier to assemble the sections of the article and complete the overall scope of the story.

We can quickly build a beginning, middle, and end to the story. Then, it's a matter of refining the details and moving content around until it's just right.

What is right? That's up to individual interpretation and taste, but each writers has their own style and voice and must work towards resonating with it. 

When I feel my article is finished, I have a sense in confidence that it both error free and contains a compelling message that other's will appreciate; perhaps even share and comment on. I want to write what I want to write and have it be read and enjoyed by others. Fortunately, I haven't written anything that has been rejected or criticized at large; perhaps I don't take enough risks or my aim it too much to please; or so that's success to me.
