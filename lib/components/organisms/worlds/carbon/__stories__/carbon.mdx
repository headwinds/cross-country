# Carbon as a Component

We breath in oxygen, exhale carbon dioxide. 

The carbon cycle is a natural process that is essential to life on Earth. Carbon is an element that is found in all living things. It is the basic building block of life and continually cycles through the environment. Carbon is stored in the atmosphere, oceans, plants, soil, and fossil fuels. 

There is a global climate crisis that is caused by the increase of carbon dioxide in the atmosphere. This is due to the burning of fossil fuels, deforestation, and other human activities. As an individual, do anything about the the carbon crisis may seem impossible. But as a collective, we can make a difference. The difference does start on the individual level. So, what can we do?

Carbon is a building block of life. It can be used to create energy, food, and products. But when carbon is released into the atmosphere, it becomes a pollutant that contributes to climate change. In order to reduce carbon, we need to support products and services that are carbon neutral or carbon negative. We need to support companies that are committed to reducing their carbon footprint. We need to support policies that reduce carbon emissions. We need to support individuals that are committed to reducing their carbon footprint.

Although ethereal, we need to start thinking about carbon as a component. What can we build with carbon on the various zoom levels of our lives? The first zoom level is the individual level; our home. We can zoom out to then consider work; the community; the city; the country; and finally the world. At each level, we can consider the products and services that are contributing to our carbon footprint; and ultimately the like-minded people that we should be connected with and work together.

Let's start with learning about the carbon cycle and how we can reduce our carbon footprint at home, work, and in our communities.

## My Carbon Footprint at home

At home, do we know how much carbon we are emitting? Are we doing anything to reduce our carbon footprint at home? Are we working with our family to reduce our carbon footprint? What products are we using that are contributing to our carbon footprint?

## Work Carbon Footprint

At work, do we know how much carbon we are emitting? Are we doing anything to reduce our carbon footprint at work? Are we working with our colleagues to reduce our carbon footprint?

## Community Carbon Footprint

At the community level, do we know who is the biggest polluter in our community? Have we elected an official that is committed to reducing the carbon footprint in our community? Are we doing anything to connect with this official and our neighbors to reduce our carbon footprint?
